# Research question

> When PMP and IOPMP individually enforce correct access-control policies, does their composition provide an end-to-end isolation guarantee for protected memory against CPU-originated and DMA-originated transactions?

Extended topics: concurrency, outstanding transactions, policy reconfiguration, reset sequencing, DMA quiescence, stale authorization, requester identity, denied-transaction side effects, and security-configuration transitions.

Negative results are valid. Do not assume a vulnerability exists.
